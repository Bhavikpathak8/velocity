Top Questions

We offer a 30-day return window for all unworn and unwashed items. Returns are free for registered members. Please visit our Returns portal to generate a label.

Standard shipping typically takes 3-5 business days. Expedited shipping options are available at checkout. You will receive a tracking number once your order ships.

Most VELOCITY footwear runs true to size. For specific performance models, we recommend sizing up half a size. Please consult the sizing chart on the product page.

Orders are processed quickly to ensure fast delivery. You have a 30-minute window to cancel or modify your order via your account dashboard before it is sent to fulfillment.
